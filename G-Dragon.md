# G-Dragon (권지용)

## Basic Information
- **Birth Name**: Kwon Ji-yong (권지용)
- **Stage Name**: G-Dragon
- **Born**: August 18, 1988
- **Birthplace**: Seoul, South Korea
- **Height**: 177 cm (5'10")
- **Blood Type**: A

## Career Overview
G-Dragon is a South Korean rapper, singer-songwriter, record producer, entrepreneur, and fashion icon. He is best known as the leader and main rapper of **BIGBANG**, one of the most influential K-pop groups of all time, formed under YG Entertainment in 2006.

### Key Milestones
- **2001**: Joined YG Entertainment as a trainee at age 12
- **2006**: Debuted with BIGBANG
- **2009**: Released first solo album *Heartbreaker* — title track won Song of the Year at MAMA
- **2012**: Released *One of a Kind* EP
- **2013**: First solo world tour "One of a Kind World Tour"
- **2017**: Released *Kwon Ji Yong* EP (USB format) — Act III: M.O.T.T.E world tour
- **2023**: Contract renewal with YG Entertainment for group activities; established own agency **Galaxy Corporation** for solo endeavors

## Artistry & Influence
- **Songwriting/Production**: Credited on 180+ songs (KOMCA); known for genre-blending (hip-hop, R&B, electronic, pop)
- **Fashion**: Global ambassador for Chanel, Saint Laurent; founded streetwear brand **PEACEMINUSONE** (2016) with Nike collaborations (Kwondo 1, Kwondo 2)
- **Cultural Impact**: Dubbed "King of K-pop"; credited with popularizing idol self-production and expanding K-pop's global reach

## Awards & Achievements
- **MAMA Awards**: 7 wins (including Song of the Year 2009, 2012)
- **Melon Music Awards**: 6 wins
- **Golden Disc Awards**: 5 wins
- **Gaon Chart Music Awards**: Multiple wins
- **Forbes**: "30 Under 30 Asia" (2017); Korea Power Celebrity #1 (2013, 2014)
- **Guinness World Records**: Most viewed YouTube video in 24 hours by a K-pop soloist ("That XX" MV teaser)

## Philanthropy
- Consistent donor to UNICEF, Sewol Ferry disaster relief, COVID-19 relief, forest fire relief
- 2019: Donated ₩100M to children's hospital via PEACEMINUSONE
- 2023: Established **Jiyong Foundation** for youth arts education

## Personal Notes
- Mandatory military service: 2018–2019 (enlisted Feb 2018, discharged Oct 2019)
- Known for: perfectionism in production, boundary-pushing visuals, private personality contrasted with bold stage persona
- Close relationships: BIGBANG members (T.O.P, Taeyang, Daesung), longtime friends from YG trainee days
